# How the SGB data changed

This history covers the documented changes to book illustrations and Data Story sources in Omeka item set 10780. It uses issue logs, snapshots, and records read back from Omeka after edits.

## September to November 2025 · From validation to live edits

The repository began in September 2025. The first validator read items and media from Omeka. By October, the validator reported missing fields, invalid values, and duplicate identifiers. In November, the team added a way to download records, clean up a copy, and preview an upload. Text copied from PDFs often contained stray spaces and hidden formatting characters. [First validator](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/3) · [Field checks](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/20) · [Offline workflow](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/29)

The earliest live upload log in the archive refers to a snapshot dated 8 November 2025. Comparison with the original snapshot shows proposed whitespace changes to 139 items and 263 media. The changes affected titles, descriptions, credits, dimensions, and other text fields. The upload log reports 802 item updates, 988 media updates, and five media failures. The update counts cover the whole batch. The exact time of the upload remains uncertain. [Earlier upload workflow](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/blob/4174d46c848ef01884f841a576ff74195893d7ca/API.md)

Later cleanup rules standardized accented characters, repaired links, and called for making placeholder media and their parent items private. A later review found that the placeholder rule checked the stored filename. The current rule also checks the original source name. [Text cleanup](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/34) · [Placeholder review](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/52)

## July 2026 · Book links, KNIL files, and item-set membership

The team applied text and link cleanup in Omeka. It replaced written citations with book DOI links when the citation clearly matched one of the nine volumes. It also corrected language values and gave a placeholder media record a title. After the team corrected an incomplete download, validation found 122 errors in the July snapshot. [Book DOI changes](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/commit/a514931) · [July edits and validation](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44)

The team replaced an old attachment on the KNIL dataset with files from the ETH Research Collection. It also corrected the dataset’s identifier. An attempt to change the detected type of two CSV attachments left that value at plain text. Their descriptive Format field already said CSV. The saved server records confirm these outcomes. In November, the team added plain-text and spreadsheet formats to the validator’s list. [Format list change](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/47)

On 10 July, the team added three existing Omeka items to set 10780 so they could appear on the research platform. It read back the three records to confirm their membership. [Membership edit log](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44#issuecomment-4933820396)

## November 2026 · Fill gaps and resolve duplicates and rights

On 4 November, the team updated 390 records. It filled empty media fields from known information, such as the parent item’s language or period and the media file type. It also standardized license names in rights text and cleaned up titles and descriptions. [Automatic edits](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44#issuecomment-6629908653)

On 18 November, the team used the picture editors’ workbooks and media filenames to check duplicate identifiers. It reassigned identifiers where different illustrations shared a number. It kept multiple files for the same object and gave them suffixes such as `_1` and `_2`. It merged confirmed duplicates and cleared old subject values from 217 records. It also corrected titles, descriptions, and dates using the parent records and source workbooks. [Identifier and subject edits](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44#issuecomment-6986741414)

On 19 November, the team aligned license links with the written rights statements and added missing licenses. That pass changed 121 media records. The worklist records the remaining decisions about sources and rights. [Rights edit log](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44#issuecomment-6986741414) · [Worklist](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44)

## November 2026 · Archive and correct the Data Story sources

The team kept a parent item for each story and assigned identifiers to the existing story records. It attached a Zenodo PDF where one was available and used a screenshot otherwise. It removed the old HTML snapshots. Reusable images, maps, charts, and datasets received separate items. The story DOI links each source back to its story. [Identifier edit log](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44#issuecomment-6986741414) · [Data Story archive record](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/50)

The team recorded rights for each archived source. It used the rights holder’s terms or a decision from the project lead to determine visibility. It preferred higher-resolution originals and excluded duplicate files, videos, and audio. On 19 November, the manifests listed 133 public sources, 35 private sources, 34 sources for review, and 28 exclusions. [Rights decisions and upload status](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/50) · [Source manifests](../../data/data_stories/)

After upload, the team removed the site assignments for 38 items that Omeka had added to two unrelated sites. It corrected 32 descriptions that referred to the wrong image or surrounding text. It replaced credit-only titles on 20 items and standardized approximate dates on three media records. The project lead approved public access to the Zenodo PDFs, including those still in copyright. [Data Story change record](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/commit/08896fb)

A final recorded pass moved extra DOIs and Handles from Identifier to Relation on story and KNIL records. It combined four descriptions on one item, corrected a language value, and completed a placeholder from the volume 3 workbook. It also kept one historical period on each of 28 items and 29 media. The validation run recorded three remaining errors, all for KNIL language values that needed a decision. [Live fixes in PR #51](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/51)

On 23 November, the project lead retired the placeholder privacy rule. Omeka needs a media record to hold the metadata of a source. Where the project may not publish a file, that record holds the placeholder image, and its metadata stays public. DSP receives these records as metadata without a file. The validator, the cleanup copy, and the restore now keep the visibility as it is. [DSP readiness record](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/issues/44)

On 24 November, the project lead separated publication from the rights check. Items are public because they hold no file. The project lead approved public Data Story sources also when their rights are not checked. Archive material from Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen SRF stays private. Private media show their metadata and a preview on the website, but no download. The rights fields describe the rights and no longer set visibility. Omeka keeps each manual visibility decision, and a rerun does not change it. Omeka still serves private files to anyone who has the address. The project accepts this risk until it retires the Omeka collection after the move to DSP. [Website change](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/forschung.stadtgeschichtebasel.ch/pull/276)

On 24 November, the team applied this rule set in Omeka. It made 172 items and 94 placeholder media public. It also made 10 real files public: one with verified rights and nine Staatsarchiv Basel-Stadt files with public domain image data. The project lead kept 16 Data Story files with unclear rights private. The 15 SRF media, two media under a ruling of 23 November, and the Data Story file of item 12869 also stay private. The change touched only the visibility, except for one record. On `ds-juedisches-basel-12`, the team removed the rights remark from the descriptions and completed the rights text. A read-back checked every value. A second preview proposed no change. After the run, set 10780 holds 1,261 public items and 34 private media. [Visibility step](https://github.com/Stadt-Geschichte-Basel/sgb-data-validator/pull/62)
